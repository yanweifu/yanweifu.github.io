[Pixel2Mesh](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.01654) was first released April 5, 2018 and appeared at ECCV 2018. Its authors include Nanyang Wang, Yinda Zhang, Zhuwen Li, Yanwei Fu, Wei Liu, and Yu-Gang Jiang. The paper describes an end-to-end framework producing triangular meshes from a single color image through graph-based deformation. The independent [ICCV 2021 paper Geometric Granularity Aware Pixel-To-Mesh, §3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/papers/Shi_Geometric_Granularity_Aware_Pixel-To-Mesh_ICCV_2021_paper.pdf) explicitly identifies Pixel2Mesh as the earliest architecture in this precise class. Keep the scope as single-RGB, end-to-end deep learning, and direct triangular-mesh output; do not claim all earlier 3D reconstruction could only produce points or voxels, or that every output is a production-ready asset. The practical distinction is avoiding the extra conversion from point/voxel representations into meshes. [Pixel2Mesh++](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.01491), first released August 5, 2019 and accepted at ICCV 2019, extends the approach to multi-view mesh generation/refinement; its [project](https://walsvid.github.io/Pixel2MeshPlusPlus/) credits Chao Wen, Yinda Zhang, Zhuwen Li, and Yanwei Fu.

Pixel2Mesh-series media links point to two dedicated technical features on PaperWeekly's official Sohu account: [Pixel2Mesh](https://www.sohu.com/a/252979433_500659), September 10, 2018, and [Pixel2Mesh++](https://www.sohu.com/a/352447127_500659), November 8, 2019.
