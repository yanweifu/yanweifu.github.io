<section id="research" class="home-section" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <h2 id="research-title">Research</h2>
  <p class="research-intro">How can models acquire new concepts, transfer knowledge, and generalize with only a few observations? This question underpins my work on <strong>learning to learn</strong>, from statistical foundations to visual and embodied intelligence.</p>
  <div class="research-grid">
    <div class="research-topic"><h3>Few-shot learning &amp; statistical foundations</h3><p>Meta-learning, sparsity, causal learning, and reliable generalization under limited supervision.</p></div>
    <div class="research-topic"><h3>Generative models &amp; 3D vision</h3><p>Diffusion models, controllable video generation, and 3D/4D reconstruction from sparse observations.</p></div>
    <div class="research-topic"><h3>Embodied intelligence</h3><p>Vision-guided manipulation, vision-language-action models, learning from demonstrations, and <a href="#egocentric-vision">egocentric &amp; ego–exo understanding</a>.</p></div>
    <div class="research-topic"><h3>Multimodal learning &amp; brain decoding</h3><p>Large-scale fMRI pretraining, fMRI-to-3D decoding, and open fMRI/EEG datasets for the research community.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
